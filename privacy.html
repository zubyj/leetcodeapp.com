Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026 · applies to Leetcode Explained 2.4.0 and later

The short version

Leetcode Explained has no accounts and no server that stores anything about you. Everything the extension keeps lives in your browser's extension storage on your device, and you can delete it by removing the extension.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: theme, display size, and which features are turned on.
  • The problem dataset bundled with the extension: video links, solution languages, company tags, and Elo ratings.
  • Your submission history: when you submit a solution on LeetCode, or use "Import history", the extension records the problem, result, language, timestamp, and runtime for each submission so it can show your progress. This is read from LeetCode using your own logged-in session and is never sent anywhere else.
  • Your last AI answer, so it is still there when you reopen the popup.

Network requests the extension makes

  • github.com: fetches solution code from the public NeetCode repository when you open the Solution code section.
  • google.com: loads small company logos (favicons) for company tags.
  • youtube.com: embeds the video player when you open the Video section. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the player.
  • leetcode.com: reads your submission results and history using your existing login, only from pages you have open.
  • AI features (only when you click Solution code or Complexity): the problem description and the code in your editor are sent to the model that answers. If "Use my ChatGPT account" is on and you are logged into chatgpt.com, the request goes to ChatGPT through your own session and OpenAI's privacy policy applies. Otherwise the text is sent to api.leetcodeapp.com, which forwards it to a hosted model and returns the answer; the prompt is not stored after the response is returned. A random, anonymous installation id and the extension version are included so abuse can be rate-limited. No account, email, or LeetCode identity is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.
  • We do not track which problems you view or solve on any server.
  • We do not read browsing activity outside leetcode.com and chatgpt.com, the only sites the extension runs on.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep the items listed above on your device.
  • tabs: to read the active tab's URL and title so the popup knows which problem you are on, and to open the extension's own pages.

This website

leetcodeapp.com uses Google Analytics to count visits. No form on this site collects personal information.
